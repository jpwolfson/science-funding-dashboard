Coverage and interpretation limits
Incomplete discovery. There is no complete federal feed of terminations, suspensions, appeals, settlements, and reinstatements. The financial detector covers only the registered accounts shown above, and sourced-event discovery covers only registered authoritative sources.
No motive inference. Transaction amounts and agency prose do not establish why an action occurred beyond a reason explicitly attributed to a source.
No legal judgment. The sentinel cannot determine whether an action is lawful, final, or likely to survive appeal.
Amounts are not interchangeable. Announced affected value, prior obligations, posted deobligations, eliminated future funding, and later restorations are separate measures and may not reconcile.
Award mapping can be partial. A portfolio announcement without award identifiers cannot be completely joined to award-level records without another authoritative source.
Routine versus extraordinary remains unresolved. Thresholds can prioritize unusual activity but cannot distinguish ordinary amendments, closeout, corrections, partial reductions, and terminations.
No comprehensive real-time docket monitoring. Registered public pages can be monitored, but immediate or complete litigation coverage may require a paid or access-controlled service.
Sources are attributed, not independently proven. Archiving an authoritative source and reporting what it says does not independently verify every factual claim in that source.
Review is optional judgment. Software can assemble evidence and propose a bounded finding; no human or agent is assigned a queue or deadline.

Generic store, detector, validation, workflow, and site	3–6 engineering days	Ordinarily none outside failures
